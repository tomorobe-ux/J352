About the Club

Founded in 1983, Die Schwaben is one of Germany's most iconic clubs. Known for beautiful football and punching above their weight. They play at the beautiful MHP Arena in front of over 60,000 fans.

VFB Stuttgart has developed several big talents like Jürgen Klinsmann, Mario Gomez, Serge Gnabry, Joshua Kimmich, Sami Khedira, Timo Werner, Nick Woltemade, Enzo Millot, and Angelo Stiller.

The club has won several major titles including five Bundesliga titles, four DFB-Pokal titles, one DFB-Supercup titles, two 2.Bundesliga titles, and two UEFA Intertoto Cups.
